# Delivereo

A 3D food delivery game set in Toronto, built for iOS with Swift and SceneKit.

You play a courier riding through a city inspired by downtown Toronto. Pick up orders from restaurants and grocery stores, deliver them to customers waiting inside their homes, and earn money and tips. Walk, sprint, drive a car, ride a bike or scooter, or hop on a TTC streetcar as a passenger.

The city includes the Financial District, Chinatown, the Eaton Centre, Scotiabank Arena, Dundas Square, BMO Field, the CN Tower and Lake Ontario. Streets have separate lanes for cars, bikes and streetcars, plus working traffic lights and crosswalks with pedestrians.

## Status

Work in progress. Built entirely in Swift with no external dependencies. All audio is synthesized at runtime and the whole city is generated in code.

## Run it

Open the project in Xcode and run it on an iPhone or the simulator. iOS 16 or later recommended.
